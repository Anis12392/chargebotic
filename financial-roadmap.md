## Overview

**What we're building:** Battery-buffered fast-charging stations for last-mile delivery robots (Earth SKU) → autonomous energy-delivery vehicle for the lunar surface (Moon SKU / THE HOPPER).

**Where we are:** Design phase. No prototype yet. Two co-founders. Pre-seed fundraising.

**What we need to raise:** **$1.5–2M pre-seed** to get to prototype, first paid pilots, and seed-raise-ready.

## Regulatory Costs (Non-Negotiable)

| Cert | Cost | Timeline | Required For |
|---|---|---|---|
| UL 9540 (ESS system cert) | $50–150K | 4–9 months | Any indoor commercial deployment |
| UN 38.3 (battery transport) | $5–7K | 2–4 weeks | Shipping batteries |
| NEC Art. 706 legal opinion | $10–15K | 1 month | AHJ compliance clearance |
| **Total** | **$65–172K** | | |

*Design constraint: keep battery buffer < 20 kWh per unit to stay below NFPA 855 indoor threshold and avoid fire suppression requirements.*

## Key Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| COGS too high to hit margin | Medium | High | Validate BOM before building pilot units (CP1) |
| UL 9540 takes 12+ months | High | Medium | Start cert early (Month 8), price pilots as pre-cert |
| No LOIs by Month 6 | Medium | Critical | Start DoorDash conversation now (Ashu Rege) |
| Pre-seed takes longer to close | Medium | High | Target 15-20 accelerators, not just Founders Inc |
| Single-source component (cells) | Medium | Medium | Qualify 2 cell suppliers in BOM phase |
| Competitor enters space | Low | High | File provisional patent before first demo |
